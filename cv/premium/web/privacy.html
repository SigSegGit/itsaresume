Moyens de paiement

Nous ne recevons ni ne conservons jamais vos numéros de carte. Le paiement est fait chez le prestataire de paiement ; si vous choisissez d'y enregistrer une carte, c'est lui qui la conserve, et nous ne gardons qu'une référence opaque, supprimée avec le compte. Vous pouvez retirer une carte enregistrée à tout moment.

  • Prestataire de paiement (responsable de traitement distinct pour la lutte contre la fraude).
  • Hébergement : serveur de l'éditeur, en France.

Means of payment

We never receive or keep your card numbers. Payment is made with the payment provider; if you choose to save a card there, the provider keeps it, and we keep only an opaque reference, deleted with the account. You can remove a saved card at any time.

Recipients

  • AI model provider (processor): receives the job offer and the profile excerpts needed for the writing, for the time of the request. Transfer outside the EU covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Payment provider (a separate controller for fraud prevention).
  • Hosting: the publisher's server, in France.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. From "My account" → "My data": download all your data (JSON), delete your profile, delete your account. For any other request: {{contact}}; answer within one month. You can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
